# 📱 Instagram Content Creation Guide for BBQ Bike

## 🎯 Content Strategy

Create a mix of content types to keep your Instagram feed engaging:

### Content Types:

1. **📸 Static Product Shots** (40%)
   - Clean studio angles
   - Detail close-ups
   - Feature highlights

2. **🎬 Rotation Videos** (30%)
   - 360° turntable animations
   - Smooth, hypnotic loops
   - Great for showcasing full design

3. **✨ Dynamic Animations** (20%)
   - Camera orbits
   - Zoom-ins on features
   - Creative transitions

4. **🎨 Lifestyle/Concept** (10%)
   - Different environments (sunset, urban, etc.)
   - Artistic lighting
   - Mood-setting content

## 📐 Instagram Dimensions

### Feed Posts:
- **Square**: 1080 x 1080 px (1:1)
- **Landscape**: 1080 x 566 px (1.91:1)
- **Portrait**: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5) - **Recommended for product shots**

### Stories/Reels:
- **Vertical**: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16)

### Profile Picture:
- **Square**: 320 x 320 px (displays as circle)

## 🎬 Video Specifications

- **Length**: 3-60 seconds (feed), 15-90 seconds (reels)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps (preferred)
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Max Size**: 100 MB (feed), 4 GB (reels)
- **Audio**: 128 kbps AAC

## 🎨 Content Ideas with Web Viewer

### Idea 1: "Feature Friday" Series
**Setup**:
- Environment: `studio`
- Auto-rotate: ON
- Duration: 10 seconds

**Process**:
1. Load model in viewer
2. Zoom into specific feature (BBQ grill, handlebars, etc.)
3. Enable auto-rotate
4. Record 10-second clip
5. Add text overlay: "Feature: [Feature Name]"
6. Post with caption explaining the feature

### Idea 2: "Environment Showcase"
**Setup**:
- Try different presets
- No auto-rotate
- Manual camera positioning

**Process**:
1. Position camera at best angle
2. Change environment preset every 2 seconds
3. Record transitions: studio → sunset → city → warehouse
4. Add caption: "BBQ Bike in every setting"

### Idea 3: "Detail Shots"
**Setup**:
- Environment: `studio` or `sunset`
- Static camera
- Zoom in close

**Process**:
1. Zoom into unique details
2. Capture high-res screenshot
3. Create carousel post with 5-10 detail shots
4. Caption: "The devil is in the details 🔥"

### Idea 4: "360° Product View"
**Setup**:
- Environment: `studio`
- Auto-rotate: ON
- Duration: 15 seconds

**Process**:
1. Position camera at 45° angle
2. Enable auto-rotate
3. Record full rotation
4. Loop seamlessly
5. Post as Reel with trending audio

### Idea 5: "Cinematic Showcase"
**Setup in Blender** (using scripts):
- Dynamic camera animation
- Sunset/golden hour lighting
- 30 seconds

**Process**:
1. Use "Camera Animation" script from BLENDER_SCRIPTS.md
2. Render high-quality video
3. Add cinematic music
4. Color grade for warmth
5. Post as Reel

## 🎨 Color Grading Tips

Make your renders pop with these adjustments:

### For Product Shots (Clean):
- Slightly increase contrast (+10-15%)
- Boost saturation slightly (+5-10%)
- Add subtle vignette for focus

### For Lifestyle/Artistic:
- Warm up shadows (add orange/yellow tint)
- Cool down highlights (add blue tint)
- Increase contrast (+20%)
- Film grain for texture

### For Dark/Moody:
- Crush blacks slightly
- Reduce overall brightness (-10%)
- Add teal to shadows, orange to highlights
- Strong vignette

## 🎥 Screen Recording Setup

### Windows:
1. Press `Win + G` to open Game Bar
2. Click record button (or `Win + Alt + R`)
3. Record your viewer session
4. Stop with `Win + Alt + R`
5. Files saved in `Videos/Captures`

### Mac:
1. Press `Cmd + Shift + 5`
2. Select recording area
3. Click "Record Selected Portion"
4. Stop from menu bar
5. Video auto-saves to Desktop

### Professional (OBS Studio):
1. Download free OBS Studio
2. Add "Display Capture" source
3. Set canvas to 1080x1080 (square) or 1080x1920 (vertical)
4. Record at 30fps
5. Export as MP4

## ✨ Pro Tips

1. **Consistency is Key**: Stick to 2-3 environment presets for a cohesive feed aesthetic
2. **Quality Over Quantity**: One amazing post beats three mediocre ones
3. **Test Everything**: Preview how your content looks in-feed before posting
4. **Engage First**: Spend 15 minutes engaging with others before posting
5. **Save Best for Reels**: Instagram pushes Reels harder than static posts
6. **Loop Perfectly**: For rotation videos, ensure the start and end match for seamless loops
7. **Add Captions**: Many people watch with sound off
8. **Cross-Promote**: Share feed posts to Stories, tease Reels in Stories
9. **Analyze**: Use Instagram Insights to see what works best
10. **Stay Authentic**: Let your passion for the BBQ Bike shine through

## 🚀 Quick Workflow Recap

1. **Open web viewer** → Upload .glb model

3. **Enable auto-rotate** for video OR position manually for photo
4. **Record/Screenshot** → Use screen capture
5. **Edit** → Add music, text, color grade
6. **Export** → 1080x1080 (feed) or 1080x1920 (reels)
7. **Post** → Add caption, hashtags, location
8. **Engage** → Respond to comments, post to Stories
