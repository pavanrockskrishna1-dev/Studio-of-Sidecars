## 🎨 Best Settings for Instagram

### Web Viewer:
- Environment: `studio` or `sunset`
- Shadows: ON
- Auto-rotate: ON (for videos)
- Background: Dark gray (#1a1a1a)

### Screen Recording:
- **Windows**: Win + G
- **Mac**: Cmd + Shift + 5
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Resolution: 1080x1080
