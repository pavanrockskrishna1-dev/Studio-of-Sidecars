- **Lights**: Key, Fill, Rim, Top
- **Result**: Realistic, professional look
- **Use for**: All your renders

- **Materials**: 4 professional materials
- **Quality**: Photorealistic
- **Use for**: Making your bike look amazing

## 🔥 Your First Post (Step-by-Step)

Let's create your first post **right now**:

### What You'll Create:
A professional 360° turntable video of your BBQ Bike

### Steps:

1. **Terminal**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

2. **Browser opens automatically** → You see the viewer

3. **Download your model**:
   - Click [this Google Drive link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1La14vaehZp2v-HFm_xSbra0jNrg4swtH/view?usp=drivesdk)
   - Click download button
   - Save the .glb file

4. **Upload to viewer**:
   - Click "Choose GLB File" button
   - Select your downloaded .glb

5. **Setup scene**:
   - Environment preset: Select `studio`
   - Background: Already set (dark gray)
   - Shadows: ON (default)

6. **Position camera**:
   - Drag to rotate view (front-right angle looks great)
   - Scroll to zoom until bike fills frame nicely
   - Slight downward angle

7. **Start rotation**:
   - Click "▶️ Auto Rotate" button (top-right)
   - Watch it spin smoothly

8. **Record** (choose your platform):
   - **Windows**: Press Win + G → Click record button → Wait 15 seconds → Stop
   - **Mac**: Press Cmd + Shift + 5 → Select area → Record → Wait 15 seconds → Stop

9. **Optional editing**:
   - Trim to perfect 15-second loop
   - Add trending audio (optional)
   - Add text overlay: "BBQ Bike 🔥" (optional)

10. **Post to Instagram**:
    - Open Instagram
    - New post → Select video
    - Square crop (1:1)
    - Caption:
      ```
      Introducing the BBQ Bike 🔥🚴
      
      Where function meets flavor.
      What do you think? 💭
      
      #BBQBike #ProductDesign #Innovation #BikeLife #BBQLife
      ```
    - Post!

**DONE! You just created and posted your first BBQ Bike content!** 🎊
