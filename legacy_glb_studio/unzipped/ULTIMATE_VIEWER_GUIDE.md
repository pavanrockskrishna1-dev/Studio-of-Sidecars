### ✋ **Drag to Move Models:**

```
1. Add your models
2. Click "✋ Enable Drag Mode" button
3. Orange message appears: "DRAG MODE"
4. Click any model and drag it!
5. Move it anywhere in 3D space
6. Click "❌ Exit Drag Mode" when done
7. Camera rotation works again
```

**While in Drag Mode:**
- Camera controls disabled (so you can drag)
- Click and hold any model
- Drag to move it
- Release to place it
- Perfect for arranging multiple models!

**After Drag Mode:**
- Click "Exit Drag Mode"
- Camera rotation enabled again
- Models stay where you placed them

## 🆘 Troubleshooting:

### **Recording not working:**
- Use Chrome or Edge browser (best support)
- Firefox also works
- Safari may have issues

### **Can't move models in drag mode:**
- Make sure "Drag Mode" is enabled (orange message shows)
- Click directly on the model (not background)
- If stuck, exit and re-enter drag mode

### **Screenshot is black:**
- Make sure models are loaded
- Camera is positioned correctly
- Try regular screenshot first, then 4K

### **Video file too large:**
- Keep recordings under 60 seconds
- Video quality is automatically optimized
- .webm format is efficient
